*A 95 percent confidence interval for the difference in loss rates, unpooled, is {fmtPct(DIFFERENCE_INTERVAL.ci[0], 1)} to {fmtPct(DIFFERENCE_INTERVAL.ci[1], 1)} percentage points a transit, Perrine-beneficiary minus all others.*

*At <ChosenAlpha /> there is convincing evidence that hulls insured to the benefit of {PERRINE_HOLDINGS} are lost on the Hundred-Day Lane at a higher rate than hulls insured to the benefit of anyone else. This finding concerns transits recorded in the Ledger between 2178 and the Register snapshot of this year. It does not identify a cause, an agent, or an intention, and no part of it should be read as doing so.*

Four claims, a scope, and a sentence at the end that says what the analysis cannot do. No adverbs. Nothing in it larger than what the procedure paid for.

Marsh comes aboard at seven with her bag already on her shoulder, because *Thessaly Dawn* has her berth slot at fourteen hundred tomorrow and a master who misses a Ceres slot waits eleven days for the next one.

She does not sit down.

"*Thessaly Ember* left Uruk High on the twenty-second of February, 2182. *Nicholson Regio* left on the twentieth. Two days apart, same office, same clerk for all I know." She puts a finger on the table, once, hard enough to be heard. "The Authority wrote *piracy* on my brother and *unknown* on theirs, and I have spent six years being told that is a distinction and not a decision."

You tell her what the brief says, because she has earned the sentence and because it will be public eventually and better from you. You tell her what it does not say, which takes longer.

<Dialogue speaker="marsh" aside="She listens to the whole thing, including the part about scope, and she does not interrupt once, which from her is a kind of courtesy nobody else aboard would recognise.">
Right. So you have proved that somebody's ships die more than mine do. Fine. That is more than anyone's managed in six years and I will not pretend otherwise.

Now. What are you going to do?
</Dialogue>

The order is already on the board behind her. *Halden Reach* is a forty-thousand-tonne Lane tender holding station a tenth of an astronomical unit sunward of the Ceres approach, and the Service does not have another one. Your sink goes into her hands for six days. Captain Ostrow, who outranks you, will brief you on an analysis of transit times that the Board says addresses your concern.

Your concern was a rate. The analysis is about means, and it arrives before the Board has convened to consider the thing it answers.

You tell Marsh you are going to go and read it.

</Scene>
