The yard's release form is one page. The shakedown report under it is nine, and Sandoval has put her card of numbers on top of both, which is her opinion of the form.

Ferrier reads at the plot with the burn timer running in the corner of the display. She reads the way she navigates: every figure checked against what produced it. "This goes to Adlinda under your name, Captain, and from Adlinda to Valhalla. I want every number in it to survive someone who wants it to be wrong." She does not say who that might be. She turns the slate toward you.
