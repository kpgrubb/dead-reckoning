<Scene stamp="MET 10/02:30" location="Bridge · burn timer running" condensed="Thirty minutes before the departure burn, Ferrier reads the shakedown report at the plot before it goes to Adlinda under your name. Every number in it has to survive a reader who wants it to be wrong.">

Nine pages of shakedown report, and the yard's release form under them, and on top of the whole stack the Chief's index card, still in pencil, held down against the free-fall drift by a magnet she has taken off something that needed it.

You have not slept. The burn timer runs in the corner of the plot display and you keep finding your eyes on it instead of the page.

Ferrier reads the way she navigates, every figure checked back against the thing that produced it, one finger on the line. She is a third of the way down page two.

"This goes to Adlinda under your name, Captain. Adlinda to Valhalla." She does not look up. "Every number in it has to survive somebody who wants it to be wrong."

She turns the slate round to face you and puts the stylus on top of it.

</Scene>
